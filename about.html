About JackLabs Six-plus years of production engineering, founded in 2014

JackLabs.ai began in 2014 with the acquisition of its first asset and internal client, the digital brand and multimedia presence Musikage. What started as a technical and creative experiment became the foundation for a modern, scalable software practice.

Formally incorporated in January 2025, JackLabs.ai unites six-plus years of production engineering with a company history that goes back to 2014: full-stack web application development, AI integration and research, and operational consulting for small to mid-sized businesses. Our background spans internal initiatives and professional contributions across industries, giving us a practical understanding of how technology drives real results.

Today JackLabs.ai operates as an ethical, AI-enabled consultancy and development studio. We design and deploy targeted, secure, and human-aligned systems that enhance productivity, improve decision-making, and strengthen technical resilience.

Our mission

To make advanced technology practical, ethical, and accessible. We bridge the gap between innovation and execution, helping organisations adopt AI and automation responsibly. Every solution we build is designed to empower people, safeguard privacy, and deliver measurable impact without unnecessary complexity or hype.

The people doing the work

Nayil R. Arana, MS

Managing Director

Nayil R. Arana, MS is the Managing Director of JackLabs.ai. His work sits at the intersection of software engineering, AI integration, and applied research. Alongside building JackLabs.ai from its roots in 2014, he has held engineering roles at leading technology companies including Atlassian and Amazon, and has developed statistical software for the Cayman Islands Monetary Authority. He now sets the firm's technical and commercial direction, advancing its mission to deliver ethical, secure, and scalable systems for modern organisations.
